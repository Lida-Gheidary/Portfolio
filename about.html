About

Hi, I’m Lida.

I help organisations understand their customers, communicate their value and turn commercial priorities into practical marketing.

My background

I bring over a decade of experience across customer research, strategy consulting and marketing transformation.

I began in marketing research and insights, working on customer behaviour, advertising effectiveness and brand questions, before moving into market-entry consulting for international companies. I later led marketing transformation across brand development, positioning, website content, social media and SEO, alongside agency-supported paid search. My work has included setting objectives and KPIs, developing marketing and sales materials, and coordinating delivery with agencies, IT and technical teams.

My focus today

Today, I combine this marketing and commercial experience with hands-on digital analytics, tracking and CRM tools, including Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and HubSpot. I use customer insight and measurement to evaluate activity and identify improvements across campaigns, content and customer journeys. My approach is to agree priorities, deliver in phases and use the results to guide the next steps.
