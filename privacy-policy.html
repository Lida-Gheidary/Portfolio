Privacy & Cookies

This page explains how this portfolio remembers your preferences and measures visits.

Your choices

Analytics and advertising storage are denied until you allow the corresponding category. You can accept optional tracking or customize your settings, where you can also reject it. Use here or in any page footer to change your choice.

Necessary preferences

Your consent choice is remembered in your browser for up to 180 days before you are asked again. Your selected light or dark theme is also remembered. These settings support the website itself and are not used for advertising.

Simple Analytics

I use Simple Analytics to count visits without analytics cookies. The pageview script loads when you open a page, including before you make a choice and after you reject optional tracking. Read what Simple Analytics collects.

Google Analytics

Google Tag Manager loads on each page. Before you choose, or if you reject analytics, Google Analytics can send cookieless measurements to Google, including consent status and information about the page visit. With analytics consent, Google Analytics may read and write measurement cookies such as _ga. The homepage banner appears when you reach Projects. You can open cookie settings yourself at any time. Read Google’s privacy information.

Marketing

No advertising or marketing tags currently run on this portfolio. A separate marketing choice is available in the preferences panel. Changes to the tools or purposes described here require a new consent version.

Changing your choice

You can withdraw optional consent at any time. The website removes its accessible optional cookies and reloads the page when needed to stop tools already loaded. Storage managed by other websites remains subject to their own controls.

External websites

Project previews and outbound links can connect you to external websites when you choose to open them. Those websites have their own privacy settings.
